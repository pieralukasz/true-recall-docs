:::note[Now part of the AI Assistant]
The standalone **Card Polish** plugin has been unified into the **[AI Assistant](/plugins/ai-assistant/)**. Rewriting and improving existing cards now runs through the assistant's *modify-card* workflow — your polish presets appear as chips in the Ask AI composer.
:::

Polishing still works the same way — simplify, tighten wording, fix formatting, split into atomic cards, or run a custom instruction — and you still reach it mid-review or while drafting in the [Flashcard Editor](/views/flashcard-editor/). To polish a card:

1. Open **Ask AI** on the card (from the review context menu or the editor's AI panel).
2. Pick a modify-card preset chip, or type what you want changed.
3. Review the proposed rewrite and **Apply** or **Reject** it.

Your existing polish presets are unchanged.
