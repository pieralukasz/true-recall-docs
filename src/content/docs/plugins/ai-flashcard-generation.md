:::note[Now part of the AI Assistant]
The standalone **AI Flashcard Generation** plugin has been unified into the **[AI Assistant](/plugins/ai-assistant/)**. Generating cards from notes, selections, and highlights now runs through the assistant's *generate-cards* workflow — your generation presets appear as chips in the Ask AI composer.
:::

AI card generation still works exactly as before; it's just reached through one place now. To generate cards:

1. Trigger **Ask AI** from the [Selection Toolbar](/views/selection-toolbar/), a review card, or the [Flashcard Panel](/views/flashcard-panel/).
2. Pick a generation preset chip (or type a free-form request).
3. Review the drafted cards and **Apply** the ones you want.

Your existing generation presets are unchanged. Configure them under [Generation Presets](/plugins/generation-presets/).
