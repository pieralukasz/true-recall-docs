:::note[Now part of the AI Workspace]
The standalone **AI Flashcard Generation** plugin is now the **Flashcard Generator**, a workflow family inside the **[AI Workspace](/plugins/ai-assistant/)**. Generating cards from notes, selections, and highlights runs through the workspace's **Generator** mode, and your generation presets are configured under `Settings → True Recall → Features → "AI Workspace" → "Generate cards"`.
:::

AI card generation still works as before; it's just reached through one place now. To generate cards:

1. Trigger **Ask AI** from the [Quick Actions Toolbar](/views/selection-toolbar/), a review card, or the [Flashcard Panel](/views/flashcard-panel/).
2. Pick a generation preset (or type a free-form request in Generator mode).
3. Review the drafted cards and **Apply** the ones you want, now or later from the [AI Inbox](/views/ai-inbox/).

Your existing generation presets are unchanged. Configure them under [Generation Presets](/plugins/generation-presets/). The Flashcard Generator requires an AI key (BYOK or Pro) and the AI Workspace feature enabled.
